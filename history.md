# Game History

This file tracks everything that's been built and changed in the game project.

---

## Session: 2026-10-03

### What the creator asked for:
A Warrior Cats game! You start as an apprentice and train to become a warrior. At the start
you design what your cat looks like and pick the first part of your name and your personality.

### What was built/changed:
- **Title screen** with a starry night forest, three cats on a rock, and fireflies
- **Cat designer**: pick fur color, markings color, pattern (solid, tabby stripes, spots,
  patches, socks), eye color, the first part of your name (40 choices, like Fire-, Storm-, Ivy-),
  and personality (Brave, Kind, Clever, Speedy, Strong, Playful, Calm, Loyal).
  There's a "Surprise Me!" button too. Your apprentice name ends in "-paw".
- **ThunderClan camp**: walk around, talk to leader Oakstar, mentor Mossfeather, Clanmates,
  the elder Mumblefoot and two kits. Oakstar makes you an apprentice when you arrive!
- **Three training lessons** from Mossfeather:
  1. Hunting: sneak up on mice and pounce (catch 5)
  2. Border Patrol: run and jump over logs, rocks and streams, marking 5 glowing trees
  3. Battle Practice: swat 12 moss balls (no claws, just practice!)
- **Warrior ceremony** under the stars: your warrior name is your name + an ending that
  matches your personality (Brave = -heart, Kind = -petal, Clever = -whisker, Speedy = -leap,
  Strong = -claw, Playful = -tail, Calm = -mist, Loyal = -song). The whole Clan cheers it!
- After the ceremony you're a bigger warrior cat in camp

### What you can do now:
- Design your cat and see your apprentice name
- Do all three trainings and find out your secret warrior name

### What's next:
- Pick a different Clan (RiverClan, WindClan, ShadowClan, SkyClan)?
- Get your own apprentice once you're a warrior
- More training: climbing trees, fishing, night patrol
- Draw your cat on paper and put it in the game

## Session: 2026-10-03 (part 2)

### What the creator asked for:
Make the art kawaii and chibi, more like the real book. Follow the main story of the first
book, but with YOU as the main character instead of Fireheart. Battle practice should be with
your mentor, and border patrol should be in a group.

### What was built/changed:
- **New chibi art**: big round heads, huge sparkly eyes, rosy cheeks, happy ^^ blinks, and
  a "Fluffy" fur option. Grumpy cats (Tigerclaw, Longtail, Yellowfang) have grumpy eyebrows!
- **Cute pastel menus**: cream and pink talking boxes with name tags
- **Book characters**: Bluestar, Tigerclaw, Lionheart, Graypaw, Ravenpaw, Spottedleaf,
  Yellowfang, Longtail, Frostfur and her kits, Sandpaw, Dustpaw, Smallear, and Smudge
- **The story, in 9 chapters**:
  1. Kittypet: you're "Biscuit" with a collar in a Twoleg garden. Smudge warns you about the forest
  2. Into the Forest: catch your very first mouse
  3. A Surprise Attack: Graypaw jumps out for a play-fight, then Bluestar and Lionheart invite
     you to join ThunderClan (and your collar comes off!)
  4. Hunting Lesson with Bluestar (Bluestar is your mentor, just like in the book)
  5. Battle Practice: spar with Bluestar. Dodge when she crouches, pounce when she's tired
  6. Border Patrol with Lionheart, Graypaw and Ravenpaw following you. Visit Tallpines,
     Sunningrocks, Snakerocks, the Owl Tree and Fourtrees, and meet a RiverClan cat at the river
  7. The Grumpy Stranger: find hurt Yellowfang and catch mice for her
  8. Rescue the Kits: ShadowClan took Frostfur's kits! Sneak through ShadowClan's camp at night,
     hiding in the ferns from the guards
  9. Warrior Ceremony: Graypaw becomes Graystripe, then you get YOUR warrior name
- Camp now looks like the book: Highrock, medicine den with ferns, gorse wall with yellow flowers
- Kid-friendly changes from the book: no cats get hurt badly, and fights are play-fights

### What you can do now:
- Play the whole story from kittypet to warrior!

### What's next:
- Book 2 adventures? (Bringing WindClan home, becoming a mentor)
- Pick a different Clan
- Draw your cat on paper and put it in the game

## Session: 2026-10-03 (part 3)

### What the creator asked for:
Go inside the dens, hunt on your own as a warrior, get your own apprentice, paint your cat with
a brush, and save slots.

### What was built/changed:
- **Save slots**: 3 slots on a new screen after the title. Each shows your cat, its name and
  how far you got. The game saves by itself as you play. Erase asks "Sure?" first.
- **Paint with a Brush!**: a new button in the cat designer. 16 colors, 3 brush sizes, eraser,
  undo and clear. The paint stays inside your cat's outline and shows up everywhere in the game.
- **Dens you can go inside**: Warriors' Den, Apprentices' Den, Nursery, Elders' Den and the
  Medicine Den. Cats nap in mossy nests (with floating Zzz's!). New book cats: Whitestorm,
  Mousefur, Darkstripe, Goldenflower, Brackenkit, Thornkit, One-eye, Halftail, Patchpelt.
  Learn about herbs in the Medicine Den, and take a nap in your own nest to have a dream.
- **Your own apprentice**: after you become a warrior, Bluestar makes Cinderkit into Cinderpaw,
  and YOU are her mentor (like Fireheart in the book). She follows you everywhere.
  Talk to her to go hunting together or teach her battle moves.
- **Warrior hunting**: walk out the gorse tunnel at the bottom of camp to hunt as much as you
  like. Your prey goes on the fresh-kill pile, which gets bigger!

### What's next:
- Cinderpaw's warrior ceremony after lots of lessons?
- Book 2: bringing WindClan home
- More dens to decorate, or a moss-ball game with the kits

## Session: 2026-10-03 (part 4)

### What the creator asked for:
A choice to be a medicine cat. Type your own name, and become leader one day. Pick your own
warrior name at the ceremony, or just go with what you get. (And: how do you get back to the
title screen?)

### What was built/changed:
- **ESC menu**: press ESC any time for "Keep playing" or "Back to title screen". The title
  screen shows "ESC = menu" now.
- **Type your own name**: in the cat designer, press SPACE on the Name row (or click "Type!")
  and type any name. "Luna" makes you Lunapaw!
- **Warrior or medicine cat?** At your apprentice ceremony, Spottedleaf says StarClan sent a
  sign, and YOU choose. Bluestar and Spottedleaf explain that medicine cats can never be leader.
- **Medicine cat story** (Spottedleaf is your mentor):
  - Chapter 4 Herb Hunting: find marigold, catmint, cobwebs and watermint. Don't touch deathberries!
  - Chapter 5 Helping Sick Cats: Clanmates tell you what's wrong, you give the right herb
  - Chapter 7: you find hurt Yellowfang and heal her with herbs instead of hunting for her
  - Chapter 9 The Moonstone: Spottedleaf gives you your medicine cat name, and a StarClan cat
    visits your dream
- **Pick your name ending**: at the warrior (or medicine cat) ceremony, say "Surprise me!" for
  your personality's ending, or "I'll pick my name!" to choose from 24 endings (-storm, -frost,
  -flower...) or type your own.
- **The road to leader** (warriors): train Cinderpaw 3 times and she becomes Cinderpelt. Then
  Lionheart retires and you become deputy. Lead 2 patrols (hunting or border patrols through the
  gorse tunnel; on border patrol Whitestorm, Graystripe and Sandpaw follow YOU). Then Bluestar
  retires and you go to the Moonstone, where 9 StarClan cats each give you a life (courage,
  kindness, hope...) and you become ___star! Back home you pick your deputy.
- **Being leader**: walk up to the Highrock to call a Clan meeting. Make Brightkit into Brightpaw
  (you pick her mentor), or get the whole Clan cheering.
- **Medicine cat after the story**: Cinderpaw becomes your medicine cat apprentice. When she's
  Cinderpelt, Spottedleaf retires and you're ThunderClan's medicine cat. Help sick cats from the
  Medicine Den, gather herbs through the tunnel, and nap for StarClan dreams.
- Retired cats (Bluestar, Lionheart, Spottedleaf) live in the Elders' Den and you can visit them

### What you can do now:
- Start a new cat, type its name, and choose the medicine cat path
- Keep playing Mosspetal: train Cinderpaw and work your way up to leader!

### What's next:
- Choose a different Clan?
- More Clan meeting ceremonies for the other kits (Thornkit, Brackenkit)
- Book 2: bringing WindClan home

## Session: 2026-10-03 (part 6)

### What the creator asked for:
Make it multiplayer, so you can friend people playing in other save slots.

### What was built/changed:
- **Friends from other slots**: everyone who plays on this computer has their own slot. Their
  cats now come to visit YOUR camp (once they've joined ThunderClan), with their own colors,
  paint and names, like Starlingstar or Cinderpelt.
- Talk to a visitor and they say hi and ask "Do you want to be friends?"
- Friendships work both ways: if Slot 1 friends Slot 2, Slot 2's cat is friends with Slot 1 too
- With a friend you can **Hunt together** (they follow you into the forest and catch mice too!),
  **Share tongues** (lots of purring and hearts), or just say hi
- The save slot screen shows each cat's friends
- Erasing a slot (or starting a new cat there) clears that slot's friendships
- Real online play with strangers isn't part of the game, to keep it safe. It's for family and
  friends who play on the same computer.

### What you can do now:
- Go to camp with one cat and look for visitors from the other slots. Make friends!

### What's next:
- Two players on the same keyboard at once?
- Give your friends gifts from the fresh-kill pile
- Friends come along on border patrols too

## Session: 2026-10-03 (part 7)

### What the creator asked for:
Two players on the same keyboard with split screen, Player 2 using W A S D. And: how do you
invite someone in?

### What was built/changed:
- **Inviting someone in**: on the title screen, Player 2 presses **E** to join
- **Play Together picking screen**: Player 1 picks a cat with LEFT/RIGHT and presses SPACE when
  ready. Player 2 picks with A/D and presses E. You each need a cat in a save slot.
- **Hunting Together** (split screen!): the left half follows Player 1, the right half follows
  Player 2, in one big forest with a pond, bushes and rocks. 75 seconds to catch as many mice as
  you can.
  - Player 1: arrows to walk, SPACE to pounce
  - Player 2: W A S D to walk, E to pounce
- At the end: who caught the most, and everyone's mice go on each cat's own fresh-kill pile.
  Playing together makes the two cats friends.
- Made Cinderpelt and Starlingstar friends (asked for by the creator)

### What you can do now:
- Get a friend or family member, press E on the title screen, and hunt together!

### What's next:
- More two-player games: a moss-ball battle, or a race along the border
- Explore the camp together in split screen

## Session: 2026-10-03 (part 8)

### What the creator asked for:
Actually be in the camp, go on patrols and interact with things, all in one world (with two players).

### What was built/changed:
- **ThunderClan Together**: a new two-player game where the camp AND the whole territory are

- **Hunting patrol**: catch mice in the forest, carry them home (your name shows how many) and
  drop them on the fresh-kill pile. They go on your own cat's pile too.
- A little map at the bottom shows where both players and the marker trees are
- On the Play Together screen, UP/DOWN (or W/S) picks the game: ThunderClan Together or the

### What you can do now:

## Session: 2026-10-04 (part 2)

### What the creator asked for:
In the two-player world: the leader can make mentors, warriors and a deputy, and do other leader
things from the books. Medicine cats can ask other cats to help collect a certain herb while they
work. And you can do things with the other player.

### What was built/changed:
- **Your own menu on your half of the screen**: Player 1 uses UP/DOWN + SPACE, Player 2 uses
  W/S + E. One player can pick things while the other keeps playing.
- **Leader's Clan meetings** (walk to the Highrock and press your key):
  - **Make a kit an apprentice**: Cinderkit, Brightkit, Thornkit or Brackenkit get their -paw
    name, and you choose their **mentor**: yourself, the other player, or a Clan warrior. If the
    mentor is one of you, the apprentice follows them around!
  - **Give a warrior name**: pick an apprentice and choose their new name from 4 choices. If the
    other player is an apprentice, they're asked "Are you ready?" first.
  - **Choose a deputy**: a Clan warrior, or the other player (if they say yes). The deputy gets a
    gold "Deputy" tag, and the leader's own game remembers who it is.
  - **Send out a patrol**: 2 or 3 Clan cats walk out of the gorse tunnel. Hunting patrols come
    back with prey for the fresh-kill pile, border patrols come back with news.
  - Cheer for ThunderClan, like before.
  - Every ceremony happens on the Highrock with the real words from the books, and the whole Clan
    cheers the new name.
- **Deputies** can send out patrols from the Highrock too.
- **Mentors**: talk to your apprentice to practice battle moves (or learn about herbs), tell
  them to wait or follow you. After 3 lessons they say they're ready for their warrior name.
- **Medicine cat work**:
  - Now and then a Clan cat gets sick and lies down in the Medicine Den, with a little cloud
    showing the herb they need. Talk to them to give it from the herb store.
  - A **herb shelf** on the medicine cat's side of the screen shows how many of each herb you have.
  - Talk to a Clan cat and ask them to **fetch a herb**. They go out and come back with 2.
  - Walk up to the other player and **ask for herbs**: they get an errand like "Find Catmint for
    Cinderpelt: 0 / 3", the herb's name shows above it in the forest, and they can pick it even
    though they're not a medicine cat. Bring it to the Medicine Den or hand it to the medicine cat.
- **Playing with the other player** (walk up to them and press your key): say something, share
  tongues (lots of purring and hearts), play **tag** (pounce to tag, 40 seconds), **race** to a
  special place (follow the arrow!), share a mouse, or give your herbs. Games ask the other player
  "Yes / Not now" first.
- The Clan **remembers** its ceremonies (new names, mentors, the deputy and the herb store) for
  the next time you play together.
- The task box at the top now shows who the deputy is, and how many sick cats you've helped.

### What you can do now:
- As the leader: go to the Highrock and make Cinderkit an apprentice with YOU as mentor, then
  choose a deputy
- As the medicine cat: wait for a sick cat, then ask your friend to find the herb they need
- Walk up to each other and play tag or have a race to Fourtrees!

### What's next:
- Kits could be born in the nursery so there are always new apprentices
- A Gathering at Fourtrees with the other Clans at the full moon
- The deputy could pick which cats go on each patrol

## Session: 2026-10-04 (part 3)

### What the creator asked for:
Not as many sick cats. And mentors should actually take their apprentices out training.

### What was built/changed:
- **Fewer sick cats**: the first one comes after about a minute and a half, then only every
  2 and a half to 3 and a half minutes, and only one sick cat at a time.
- **Clan mentors go training**: every so often a mentor and their apprentice (like Ravenwing and
  Brightpaw, or Tigerclaw and Thornpaw) walk out of the gorse tunnel together, down the path to
  the **Training Hollow**, practice pounces and say things like "Watch my paws!", then walk home.
  Mentors napping in their den wake up for it and go back to bed after. Each trip is a lesson.
- **Take your own apprentice out training** (talk to them):
  - **Battle training at the Training Hollow**: an arrow shows the way. When you both get there,
    you practice pouncing, dodging and back-kicks together.
  - **Hunting lesson in the forest**: catch 2 mice with your apprentice close by. Then they catch
    one too, and you carry it home!
  - **Herb lesson** (for medicine cat apprentices): pick 2 herbs with them close by, then they find
    one by themselves.
  - Every lesson counts. After 3 lessons your apprentice is ready for their warrior name.

### What you can do now:
- Go to the Training Hollow (down the path from camp) and watch the Clan train
- Make a kit your own apprentice and take them hunting

### What's next:
- Kits could be born in the nursery so there are always new apprentices
- A Gathering at Fourtrees with the other Clans at the full moon

### What the creator asked for:
New kits should be born often (we ran out of kits!), and the camp should be bigger.

- **New kits are born**: about every minute or so, Frostfur or Goldenflower has a new kit in the

  (The one-player camp is the same as before.)

### What you can do now:

## Session: 2026-10-04 (part 5)

### What the creator asked for:
Send out more than one patrol at a time in ThunderClan Together, and make the cats spread out
inside and outside the camp.

### What was built/changed:
- **Lots of patrols at once**: the leader (or deputy) can now have up to 3 patrols out at the
  same time. After sending one, you're asked "Send out another patrol?" Each patrol is named after
  the cat leading it, like "Tigerclaw's hunting patrol is heading out!"
- **You can watch the patrols**: they walk out of the gorse tunnel in a line and go around the
  camp wall. Border patrols go to a special place (Fourtrees, the Owl Tree, Tallpines,
  Sunningrocks or Snakerocks). Hunting patrols go into the forest and pounce on prey. Then they
  come home with prey or news.
- The task box at the top shows how many patrols are out.
- **The camp is spread out**: every warrior and apprentice has their own spot on the sand, all
  over the camp, so the cats aren't squished into the middle anymore.
- **Cats out in the territory**: some cats are already out when you start, and more go out
  every so often. They sun on Sunningrocks, hunt in the forest, look up at the Owl Tree, wave at
  RiverClan by the river, and then walk home. Walk up to them to hear what they're doing!

### What you can do now:
- Go to the Highrock, send out a hunting patrol AND a border patrol, then follow one!
- Explore the forest and find Clanmates out at the special places

### What's next:
- A Gathering at Fourtrees with the other Clans at the full moon
- Kits could play moss-ball with you
- You could choose which cats go on each patrol
