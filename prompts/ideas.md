# Game Ideas to Get You Started!

Not sure what to make? Try saying one of these to get started.
You can always change things later!

---

## Starter Ideas

**"Make a platformer where I'm a cat jumping on clouds"**

**"I want a game where I catch falling stars but dodge the rocks"**

**"Make a racing game where I'm a turtle trying to beat a rabbit"**

**"I want to explore a jungle and find hidden treasure"**

**"Make a game where I'm a chef and I have to catch the right ingredients"**

**"I want a space game where I fly a rocket through asteroids"**

---

## After Your Game is Running

Try saying things like:

- "Make the character jump higher"
- "Add a bad guy that walks back and forth"
- "I drew my character on paper! I put the picture in the drawings folder"
- "Add a score counter"
- "Make it so I win when I collect 10 coins"
- "Add a second level"
- "Make the background blue like the sky"
- "Add a sound when I jump"
- "Make the enemies faster"
- "Add a title screen"

---

## Tips

- **Be creative!** There are no wrong ideas
- **Start simple** — you can always add more later
- **Draw your characters!** Use a dark marker on white paper,
  take a photo, and put it in the `my-drawings` folder
- **Have fun!** This is YOUR game
